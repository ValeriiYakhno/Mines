    How to play

    1. 1Pick the number of mines, the field size and your bet, then press Play.
    2. 2Open tiles. Every safe tile raises the multiplier, a mine ends the round.
    3. 3Press Cash out after the first safe tile to take the current win.
    4. 4More mines means a higher multiplier per tile and a higher risk.
